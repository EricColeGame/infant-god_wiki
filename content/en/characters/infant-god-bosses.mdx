## Who Are the Infant God Bosses in Cult of the Lamb?

When you conquer The One Who Waits (Narinder) at the end of the main campaign, your ascension to godhood creates ripples across the cosmic ether. However, as the hooded Mystic Seller quickly reminds you, you are merely an "infant god"—inexperienced, artless, and leaving chaotic spiritual residue behind. 

The original Bishops of the Old Faith—Leshy, Heket, Kallamar, and Shamura—did not receive a peaceful passage into death. Instead, their trapped souls linger between realms, forced to relive their final moments.


To pacify the boundary between worlds, the player must re-enter each altered realm, slay the enhanced minibosses, and defeat the resurrected Bishop forms. These challenging encounters represent your rite of passage from an upstart deity into a true sovereign of death.

## The Role of the Mystic Seller (???)

The Mystic Seller is an ancient cosmic merchant whose existence dates back to the initial formation of the Lands of the Old Faith. Standing in front of the gateway to the final arena, this galaxy-cloaked entity acts as both a harsh mentor and a premier endgame vendor for the **Infant God bosses** storyline.

According to developer insights and community lore dissections on the [Cult of the Lamb official Steam hub](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1313140/Cult_of_the_Lamb/), this cosmic wanderer traverses planes to trade with deities across millennia.

```
         ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
```

### Wheel of Fate Drop Table Mechanics

Once you bring your first God Tear to the entity, they will enter a formal business contract with you, even allowing you to bestow a custom name upon them. You can spin their prize wheel by exchanging God Tears collected from minibosses and Bishops.

| Reward Category | Base Drop Probability | Exhaustion Condition | Replacement Item |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forgotten Commandment Stone** | ~40% Weight | Caps at 24 stones (28 with DLC expansions) | Evenly redistributed to other active pool items |
| **Exclusive Follower Forms** | Split evenly (~20%) | All unique forms unlocked (e.g., Koala, Shrimp) | Necklaces / Talisman Fragments |
| **Unique Cult Decorations** | Split evenly (~20%) | All blueprints acquired | Necklaces / Holy Talismans |
| **Specialized Necklaces** | Split evenly (~20%) | Infinite availability (Light, Dark, Golden, etc.) | Remains permanently in pool |

*Player experience tip*: If you are hunting for specific follower forms or rare necklaces (such as the Light and Dark necklaces used for Baal and Aym rituals), you can save your game directly before spinning the wheel and reload if you don't receive your desired item.

## Strategy: Conquering the Rematch Encounters

Facing down the resurrected Bishops requires a different mindset than the standard crusade runs. In the post-game phase, the encounters scale up in health pools, attack frequency, and environmental hazards.

```
Preparation Stage:
├─ Equip High-Mobility Fleeces (e.g., Fleece of the Fates / Fleece of the Berserker)
├─ Pack High-Damage Curses (Hounds of Fate, Divine Guardian for i-frames)
└─ Stack Follower Combat Boosts at the Temple
```

To easily dispatch these **Infant God bosses**, consider the structured tactical guidelines below:

### Combat Phases & Counter-Strategies

| Encounter | Primary Threat | Tactical Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Leshy Rematch** | Screen-wide shockwaves & worm adds | Use *Divine Guardian* for invulnerability frames while attacking point-blank. Prioritize clearing spawned adds immediately. |
| **Heket Rematch** | Rapid multi-directional fireballs & leaping | Maintain medium distance. Bait her leaps into corner walls, then punish during the recovery animation with heavy melee swings. |
| **Kallamar Rematch** | Dense bullet-hell rings & sword slashes | Bring a high-level projectile curse or a weapon with reflection properties. Never stay stationary in the center ring. |
| **Shamura Rematch** | Unpredictable aerial drops & razor thread | Watch floor shadow indicators closely. Roll forward through Shamura's landing spots rather than backward to exploit blind spots. |

## Unlocking Purgatory and Endless God Tear Farming

Once you defeat your first Bishop during the post-game crusade, the Mystic Seller unlocks **Purgatory**. Purgatory is an alternate dimension where time stands completely still in your cult village, allowing you to crusade without letting your followers starve or lose faith.

```
Step 1: Enter Purgatory Daily Gateway
Step 2: Choose Boss Rush or Dungeon Run Course
Step 3: Clear Rooms with Fixed Fleeces & Weapon Sets
Step 4: Collect God Tears and Trade with Mystic Seller
```

### Purgatory vs. Standard Crusade Comparison

| Feature | Standard Post-Game Crusades | Purgatory Runs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time Passage** | In-game clock progresses | In-game village time is frozen |
| **Death Penalty** | Lose a portion of gathered loot | Instant run failure, retain collected God Tears |
| **Run Structure** | Multi-layer branching map | Structured Boss Rush or Room Gauntlet |
| **Daily Limit** | Unlimited runs per in-game day | 1 successful attempt per in-game day |
| **Primary Incentive** | Cult materials, followers, seeds | Pure God Tear farming & combat testing |

Taking down the **Infant God bosses** across both standard crusades and Purgatory gauntlets ensures a steady supply of God Tears, allowing you to rapidly complete your doctrine book via Forgotten Commandment Stones.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes the Infant God bosses different from early-game bosses?
The **Infant God bosses** feature modified attack patterns, significantly higher health pools, tighter projectile spreads, and faster recovery windows. Slaying them also awards God Tears rather than standard hearts or basic resources.

### Can you fail the Mystic Seller questline if a Bishop dies in your cult?
No. Once recruited, former Bishops behave like standard followers (with unique appearances and traits). Even if they perish from old age, illness, or sacrifice, you can simply resurrect them using the Resurrection Ritual at your cult's temple altar.

### Why is the player called an "infant god"?
The Mystic Seller refers to The Lamb as an "infant god" because, despite wielding the Red Crown and vanquishing Narinder, The Lamb has only recently stepped into divine authority. The dialogue emphasizes that true godhood requires maintaining cosmic balance, shepherding souls into the afterlife, and commanding universal respect across planes.

### What should I prioritize buying from the God Tear prize wheel?
In the early post-game, prioritize obtaining all **Forgotten Commandment Stones**. These allow you to enact every opposing doctrine you skipped during the main campaign, giving your cult massive passive efficiency and morale buffs. Once doctrines are maxed out, pivot your focus toward rare follower forms and the special Light and Dark necklaces.
