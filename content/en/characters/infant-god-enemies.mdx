## Combat Strategies for Defeating Infant God Enemies

Tackling **Infant God enemies** requires a deliberate approach focused on status mitigation and mobility. Unlike conventional humanoid bosses, these foes often manipulate stage geometry and summon waves of defensive minions.

Never leave support summons unattended. In many instances, the central boss absorbs energy from nearby adversaries, granting damage immunity or health regeneration. Use stun abilities, freeze spells, or wide sweep attacks to clear the perimeter before focusing on the primary target.

### 2. Equip Holy and Void Resistances
Because these battles combine divine light with corrupted void energies, hybrid defensive gear provides the best survivability. Stacking raw physical armor will leave you vulnerable to devastating elemental bursts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes Infant God enemies unique compared to standard bosses?
These encounters combine delicate lore motifs of innocence and divine power with aggressive arena-control mechanics. Rather than relying solely on direct melee strikes, they test your ability to handle multi-layered bullet patterns, minion summons, and phased invulnerability windows.

### What is the most effective damage type against Infant God enemies?
Corrupted or void-aligned variations are typically weakest to physical strike damage and concentrated pure elemental attacks. Standard holy damage is generally ineffective due to their innate divine heritage.

### How do I avoid the arena-wide wail attack?
Watch for the glowing aura telegraph around the boss. As soon as the light pulses outward, perform an invulnerability frame (i-frame) dodge roll directly toward the wave, or seek shelter behind the stone pillars scattered around the arena perimeter.

### Why do summoned adversaries keep respawning during the fight?
Summons will continue to spawn if the primary entity maintains full energy channels. Focus on breaking the boss's posture or posture gauge to disrupt the summoning ritual, allowing you to finish off remaining adds permanently.
