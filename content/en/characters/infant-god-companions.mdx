## Tactical Combat Guide: Utilizing Infant Abilities

In high-tier encounters, managing an infant god's emotional state—frequently governed by a **Harmony Meter**—dictates victory. If the juvenile deity takes unmitigated hits, their stress builds up, leading to uncontrolled divine backlash. Player experience shows that keeping their stress levels low stabilizes outgoing party buffs.

### Managing the Harmony System

* **Calm State (0–30 Stress):** Companion maintains steady healing and protective shields. Aura efficiency increased by 15%.
* **Focused State (31–70 Stress):** Ideal combat window. Offensive divine abilities gain a 25% critical strike rate.
* **Overwhelmed State (71–100 Stress):** Companion stops listening to tactical commands and unleashes a wide-area shockwave that damages both enemies and allies before temporarily entering a dormant state.

```
[0% Stress] ─── Stable Auras ─── [50% Stress] ─── Peak Crit ─── [100% Stress: Backlash]
```

### Practical Combat Rotation Example
1. **Opener:** Cast *Aegis of the Cradle* to protect your **Infant God companions** from early ranged attacks.
2. **Setup Phase:** Group enemy units using frontline crowd control while the deity charges their environmental resonance.
3. **Burst Phase:** Trigger the deity's ultimate ability when their Harmony Meter sits between 50% and 65% for maximum damage scaling without triggering backlash.
4. **Recovery:** Rotate backline cleanses to wipe any negative status effects off the companion, preserving their spirit efficiency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes Infant God companions unique compared to standard familiars?
Unlike ordinary familiars or summoned pets, divine infant companions possess autonomous scaling mechanics, dynamic auras, and mood-based combat performance. Their abilities scale directly with party devotion and divine favor rather than standard weapon stats, making them versatile utility and support powerhouses.

### How do I prevent my companion from triggering a Divine Backlash?
Maintain active threat management with a dedicated tank character to keep enemies off the juvenile deity. Additionally, use soothing consumables like Consecrated Incense and cycle defensive barriers to keep their Harmony stress meter safely below the 70% danger threshold.

### Which character classes have the best synergy with Infant God companions?
Paladins, Clerics, and Enchanters naturally synergize with these companions because their defensive shields and aura-buffing talents amplify the deity's innate spirit scaling. Heavy crowd-control classes also work well by grouping targets for the deity's wide-area divine bursts.

### Can Infant God companions be permanently lost in permadeath modes?
In most tactical rulesets, juvenile deities cannot be permanently slain due to their immortal lore characteristics. Instead, reaching zero health causes them to return to a dormant spirit slumber, disabling their aura passives until the party rests at a designated shrine or sanctuary.
