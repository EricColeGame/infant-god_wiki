## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most important Infant God features to prioritize early?
In the early stages, prioritize **Sanctuary Resonance** and **Inherent Trust**. These two stats ensure that your godling gains passive experience safely while minimizing the risk of ability failure or agitation during field exploration.

### Can an Infant God permanently die during combat?
In most standard configurations, the entity does not suffer permanent death. Instead, taking critical damage forces the Infant God back into a dormant sanctuary state, incurring a temporary trust debuff and halting essence generation until restored.

### How does the trust mechanic influence Infant God features?
Trust directly dictates how reliably the entity executes companion miracles. High trust leads to proactive shields and timely crowd control, whereas low trust causes erratic retreats during intense combat phases.

### Can I reset the evolutionary path of my Infant God?
Yes. Resetting requires an item known as the *Tear of Rebirth*, which refunds all invested Primordial Ambrosia and allows you to respec your companion's core domain tree from the Toddler stage onward.
