## Overview of Core Encounter Systems

The battle against the Infant God is structured around resource management, spatial awareness, and threshold management. Unlike traditional encounters where brute-force DPS can skip mechanics, this boss features mandatory phase transitions tied to both remaining health pools and active arena hazards.

To survive, players must track boss stance transitions, ground-targeted debuffs, and arena-wide gauge meters. Community reports highlight that neglecting the boss’s latent energy bar leads to irreversible wipe conditions during transitions.

## Phase 1: The Dormant Awakening

In the opening phase (100% to 75% HP), the boss establishes baseline attack routines. The initial **Infant God mechanics** test your party's positioning discipline and ability to mitigate consistent physical cleaves. 

During this stage, the boss remains stationary at the center of the arena, casting sweeping frontal attacks and periodically marking players with delayed-burst markers.

### Phase 1 Attack Patterns and Responses

| Ability Name | Cast Time | Telegraph Visual | Counterplay & Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Astral Slap** | 1.2s | Sweeping gold arc in front | Main tank turns boss away; ranged stays behind. |
| **Cradle Shard** | Instant (5s fuse) | Blue glowing circle under player | Affected player runs to outer perimeter before detonation. |
| **Wail of the Newborn** | 3.5s | Pulsing white ring across arena | Group defensive cooldowns; stack near healers for splash heals. |
| **Cosmic Crawl** | 2.0s | Linear ground tremor | Step out of the frontal directional path immediately. |

### Tactical Checklist for Phase 1
* **Positioning:** Anchor the boss facing North. All DPS and healers should remain positioned between South-West and South-East.
* **Debuff Stacking:** Ensure high uptime on armor shred and elemental exposure within the first 15 seconds.
* **Energy Management:** Keep the boss's internal charge gauge below 80% by breaking the resonance pillars as soon as they spawn on the arena edges.

## Phase 2: Cosmic Nursery & Spatial Control

Pushing the boss below 75% triggers the second phase, introducing spatial tethering and minion spawns. Here, **Infant God mechanics** become significantly more punitive, requiring strict split-target coordination.

The arena is divided into three distinct concentric rings. Stepping across ring boundaries while afflicted with the *Cosmic Tether* debuff will trigger catastrophic party-wide damage.

## Phase 3: Apotheosis (Soft Enrage)

At 30% HP, the boss enters its final, empowered state. All previous ground hazards clear, but the arena begins shrinking by 10% every 30 seconds. In this phase, navigating the **Infant God mechanics** becomes a pure race against incoming damage and dwindling floor space.

The boss gains a permanent stacking buff, *Ascendant Fury*, increasing all outgoing damage by 4% every 10 seconds.

### Phase 3 Survival and Burst Windows

| Boss Health % | Stack Count | Boss Damage Bonus | Strategic Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **30% - 25%** | 1 - 3 | +4% to +12% | Re-apply all long-term debuffs; align offensive burst cooldowns. |
| **24% - 15%** | 4 - 7 | +16% to +28% | Rotate major external damage mitigation on the main tank. |
| **14% - 5%** | 8 - 10 | +32% to +40% | Use Bloodlust/Heroism effects; ignore non-essential mechanics. |
| **< 5%** | 11+ | +44%+ (Deadly) | Full burn; rely on personal invulnerabilities and panic shields. |

During Apotheosis, execution speed is paramount. High mobility classes should bait the dropping *Starlight Meteors* near the edge of the shrinking perimeter so that melee damage dealers maintain uninterrupted uptime on the boss's central hitbox.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most lethal Infant God mechanics to watch out for?
The most lethal mechanics are the **Astral Umbilicus** tether in Phase 2 and the shrinking boundary during Phase 3 **Apotheosis**. Mismanaging player tether distances causes instant party-wide lethal bursts, while hesitation in Phase 3 leads to running out of viable floor space.

### Can you skip phases with burst damage?
No. The boss possesses hard invulnerability gates at 75% and 30% health. Pushing damage past these thresholds will immediately force the transition sequence, consuming excess damage into the transition shield.

### How do you counter the Cosmic Tether in Phase 2?
Linked players must remain in the same concentric ring and maintain a steady 8-to-12 meter separation. Moving too close triggers a lightning chain to nearby allies, while moving too far snaps the link violently, causing massive raid damage.

### What is the ideal party composition for clearing Infant God mechanics?
An ideal standard composition consists of two tanks (one dedicated main tank and one mobile off-tank), two healers (one strong burst healer and one proactive shield buffer), and four high-mobility single-target DPS classes.
