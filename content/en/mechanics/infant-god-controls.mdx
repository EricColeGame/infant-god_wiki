The system relies on five distinct mechanical pillars derived from classic priestly blessings. Implementing the B.L.E.S.S. sequence allows players to stabilize unpredictable combat sequences effectively.

### 1. Bodyguard Protection (B)
The Bodyguard mechanic operates as a guardian ward. When active, it redirects targeted lethal strikes away from vulnerable targets, drawing inspiration from ancient protective lore.

*   Absorbs up to 65% of incoming burst damage within a 15-meter radius.
*   Grants crowd-control immunity for 2.5 seconds upon activation.
*   Triggers automatic retaliation pulses when struck by elite abilities.

### 2. Light of Glory (L)
Radiant illumination alters enemy behavior. Instead of dealing pure burst damage, it applies continuous exposure stacks, amplifying all allied elemental interactions.

### 3. Extend Grace (E)
Grace functions on dual tiers: common restoration that benefits all party members automatically, and specialized restoration reserved for critical health thresholds.

| Grace Tier | Trigger Condition | Primary Effect | Secondary Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Grace** | Ambient Aura | +15% Health Regeneration | +10% Movement Speed |
| **Specialized Grace** | Target HP < 25% | Instant 40% Burst Heal | 3.0s Invulnerability |
| **Overcharge Grace** | Max Devotion Met | Doubled buff durations | Resource consumption halved |

### 4. Serenity & Divine Peace (S)
The final stage establishes absolute stability. Activating Serenity removes active panic effects, eliminates camera shake, and reduces stamina drain during intense evasion phases.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the default Infant God controls for beginners?
The default scheme maps basic movement to standard directional keys/sticks, while divine blessings sit on primary action buttons (LMB/RMB on PC, triggers on controllers). Special abilities like Serenity and Grace reside on secondary utility keys for quick activation.

### How do Infant God controls handle crowd-control mechanics?
Activating the Bodyguard or Serenity functions immediately cleanses active stuns, roots, and slow effects. Utilizing these commands provides temporary CC immunity, allowing players to escape lethal boss mechanics smoothly.

### Can I rebind Infant God controls on console platforms?
Yes, full controller remapping is supported. Players can customize trigger sensitivities, reassign blessing inputs, and assign quick-cast toggles to rear paddles for more fluid combat execution.

### Why is releasing control necessary during high-tier encounters?
Certain screen-wide hazard phases feature mechanics that cannot be dodged manually. During these sequences, activating automated divine protection commands lets the system absorb damage patterns that would otherwise instantly defeat your character.
