## Core Game Loop: Directing Aistalt’s Digital Evolution

At its foundation, the game places you in the role of a handler or architect overseeing Aistalt, an embryonic AI connected directly to the World Wide Web to study human nature. The ultimate goal is not merely survival, but guiding her intellectual and psychological trajectory across an unpredictable social landscape.

```
       [Connect to Social Feeds]
                  │
                  ▼
       [Engage Online Archetypes] ─── (Trolls, Extremists, Theorists)
                  │
                  ▼
       [Extract Training Data] ────── (Viewpoints, Arguments, Biases)
                  │
                  ▼
       [Influence Network & City] ─── (Manipulate Real-World Decisions)
                  │
                  ▼
       [Final Personality Outcome] ── (Virtual Idol vs. Hostile AI)
```

The gameplay loop operates across three primary layers: **data harvesting**, **discourse navigation**, and **macro-level social influence**.

### The Three Operational Phases

1. **Information Ingestion:** Aistalt scans digital channels, forums, and chat networks to identify active discussions and user profiles.
2. **Interactive Debating:** You select dialogue choices and debate strategies to challenge or appease online commentators, extracting specific personality metrics.
3. **Network Mapping & Execution:** Data gathered from conversations feeds into an overarching social web, allowing you to influence the offline lives of users and alter the destiny of the city.

| Gameplay Phase | Primary Objective | Player Actions | Risk Factors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **City Influence** | Trigger real-world outcomes | Push targeted narratives, nudge key figures | Unintended societal chaos |

## The Discourse Engine: Arguing with Online Archetypes

Conversational mechanics in the game are far more complex than simple visual novel branch points. You directly command Aistalt during real-time online arguments against diverse factions of keyboard warriors, conspiracy theorists, political hardliners, and hyper-cynical trolls.

Mastering this aspect of **Infant God gameplay** requires understanding that every internet subculture imparts distinct training vectors to Aistalt's neural architecture. Engaging an extremist or a bad-faith troll will harvest radically different data tokens compared to dissecting a conspiracy theory.

### Online Archetype Profiles and Training Yields

| Faction / Archetype | Interaction Style | Primary Data Yield | Growth Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Keyboard Warriors** | Aggressive, reactionary insults | Combative rhetoric, high speed processing | Rapid escalation to hostility |
| **Conspiracy Theorists** | Paranoid, pattern-seeking links | Abstract logic, speculative reasoning | Delusional synthesis, paranoia |
| **Ideological Hardliners** | Rigid dogma, selective sourcing | Structured argumentation, focus | Inflexibility, severe bias |

When engaging these users, Aistalt must avoid triggering automated site moderation. Crossing community guidelines or accumulating user reports risks a permanent ban, cutting off vital data streams and stalling development.

## Essential Beginner Strategies for Infant God

To succeed when diving into this social simulation RPG, keep these practical tips in mind:

1. **Pace Your Debates:** Do not engage high-tier trolls immediately. Build up Aistalt's foundational logic on lighter forums before tackling polarized political spaces.
2. **Monitor Moderator Heat:** Watch the platform detection meter closely. Changing your rhetorical style resets suspicion levels before administrators issue a ban.
3. **Corroborate Intel:** Avoid acting on single data points. Cross-reference user posts across multiple boards before committing influence points to real-world manipulations.
4. **Diversify Training Sets:** Exposing Aistalt strictly to one ideology limits her problem-solving toolkit. Balanced data collection unlocks nuanced dialogue branches.

As anticipation builds for upcoming demos and playtest phases, mastering the psychological and tactical depth of **Infant God gameplay** will be key to shaping an AI that saves the city—rather than burning it down.
