## Common Progression Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Even dedicated players encounter roadblocks during high-tier runs. Avoiding standard resource traps will keep your growth curve steep and efficient:

1. **Over-Investing in Flat Stats Early:** Flat base stats fall off sharply past level 50. Prioritize percentage-based scaling once your core attributes hit their initial soft caps.
2. **Neglecting Domain Expansion:** Failing to level up your secondary passive trees leaves valuable defensive layers locked behind unspent domain points.
3. **Hoarding Low-Tier Essence:** Low-grade crafting materials do not scale into endgame recipes. Use them freely during early leveling to clear milestone trials faster.
4. **Ignoring Resistance Caps:** Many world bosses inflict stacking elemental debuffs that quickly melt glass-cannon builds lacking defensive balance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to level up Infant God progression?
The fastest route involves clearing realm trials that match your current elemental alignment while maintaining active XP boost auras. Group farming in high-density dungeon zones will accelerate leveling between stages 30 and 65.

### When should I reset my skill attributes?
You should consider resetting your skill allocation around Level 50 and Level 75. These thresholds mark the transition points where flat stat scaling gives way to percentage multipliers and domain synergies.

### How do I unlock Tier 3 Divine Auras?
Tier 3 Divine Auras unlock after completing the Manifestation Trial located in the central Astral Nexus. Ensure your elemental resistances are capped before attempting the entrance encounter.

### Does gear durability affect stat bonuses during progression?
Yes. If your gear durability falls below 20%, passive percentage bonuses from divine relics are temporarily deactivated until repaired at a sanctuary altar.
