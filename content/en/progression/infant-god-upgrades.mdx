## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Infant God upgrades?
**Infant God upgrades** are core progression nodes and divine skill enhancements unlocked during the early-to-mid stages of character development. They provide scaling damage multipliers, survival shields, and mechanical utility essential for overcoming progression bottlenecks.

Yes, most progression systems allow players to reset allocated points via respec tokens or in-game currency. However, it is advisable to plan your route carefully to avoid wasting resources on frequent reallocations.

### When should I transition from flat stat nodes to percentage multipliers?
Transition toward percentage-based nodes once your baseline gear provides sufficient raw stats—typically around Tier 3 (character level 40+). Prioritizing percentage nodes too early yields smaller returns due to lower base stats.
