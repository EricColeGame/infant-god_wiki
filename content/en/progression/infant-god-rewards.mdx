## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most valuable Infant God rewards to target first?
The most valuable rewards are the **Crown of Primordial Will** and the **Primordial Core** crafting materials. The crown provides an immediate surge in burst holy damage, while the cores allow you to forge endgame radiant weapons essential for higher mythic tiers.

### Can you solo-farm the Infant God encounter for rewards?
Yes, players with endgame gear ratings can solo Apprentice and Veteran difficulties. However, Heroic and Ascended tiers generally require a balanced group or hyper-optimized self-sustain builds due to the stacking radiance debuff applied in Phase 3.

### How do weekly lockout resets affect Infant God rewards?
Direct equipment drop chances from the main chest reset on weekly server maintenance. While you can continue running the boss for baseline Divine Ash and non-curated materials, high-tier mythic drops are limited to one reward claim per character per weekly lockout cycle.
