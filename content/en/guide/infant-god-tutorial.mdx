The Infant God represents an ascended archetype characterized by rapid resource generation, high base burst damage, and evolving deity passives. Unlike conventional divine casters, this class scales inversely with damage taken, converting early combat vulnerability into devastating retribution phases.

## Skill Rotations and Combat Strategies

Mastering the rhythm of spell weaving is fundamental. This **Infant God tutorial** breaks combat down into three repeatable phases: Preparation, Burst Window, and Reset.

### Ideal Ability Rotation Order

1. **Pre-Cast Shielding:** Activate *Gilded Aegis* 2 seconds before entering boss range to build immediate initial poise.
2. **Faith Generation:** Cast *Holy Flash* twice to generate baseline *Faith* stacks quickly.
3. **Ascension Phase:** Trigger *Ascendant Burst* when the target exposes a weak point or enters a stagger state.

## Advanced Endgame Optimization Tactics

Once you cross into high-tier raid encounters, flat stat scaling yields diminishing returns. Mastering micro-mechanics allows you to pull ahead on damage meters while maintaining unmatched team utility.

### Pro Tips for High-Level Encounters

- **Animation Canceling:** You can cancel the final backswing frame of *Holy Flash* by instantly initiating a sidestep, cutting channel downtime by nearly 0.3 seconds per cast.
- **Poise Stacking:** Never enter the ascension phase without active damage reduction buffs. Player experience shows that getting interrupted mid-cast resets your gauge without refunding resources.
- **Boss Tethering:** Keep yourself within mid-range distance (8–12 meters). Being too far decreases aura tick rates, while point-blank positioning leaves you susceptible to heavy melee cleaves.

Applying the advanced methods outlined in this **Infant God tutorial** guarantees consistent clears across all mythic-level difficulty tiers.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes this Infant God tutorial different from standard caster guides?
Unlike traditional mages or clerics, the Infant God utilizes an inverse scaling model that converts incoming pressure into Faith-based counter mechanics. This **Infant God tutorial** focuses on maximizing risk-reward management rather than passive backline spellcasting.

### What is the most common mistake new players make?
The most frequent mistake is hoarding Faith points instead of spending them on cooldown. Storing resources without triggering *Ascendant Burst* leads to substantial DPS loss over prolonged dungeon fights.

### Can the Infant God play as a dedicated support or healer?
While the class possesses defensive utility such as *Cradle Shield*, its primary design centers around burst-oriented hybrid damage. Building purely for healing strips away the unique offensive passives that define the archetype.

### Which secondary stat should I prioritize after capping Divine Spark?
Once you hit your initial Divine Spark cap, invest heavily into *Aether Flow*. High cooldown recovery allows seamless transitions between defensive resets and burst phases without awkward resource downtime.
