## Who Is the Mystic Entity Addressing the "Infant God"?

After defeating The One Who Waits, the doorway to the final arena is replaced by an enigmatic vendor officially designated as `???` (often called the Mystic Seller). Draped in a cosmic cloak flowing with starfields and leaking dark ichor, this merchant claims to have existed since the world began. 

The vendor immediately mocks your inexperience, labeling you an "infant god" who has slain the Old Faith bishops without granting their spirits eternal rest. To correct this imbalance, you enter a divine contract: revisit the four major realms, defeat the enhanced bishops, and trade ancient currency known as God Tears for divine treasures.

## Complete Infant God Walkthrough: Step-by-Step Progression

Ascending from an inexperienced deity to the undisputed God of Death requires completing a strict sequence of objectives. Follow the step-by-step roadmap below to clear the main post-game storyline.

```
Defeat The One Who Waits ➔ Meet Mystic Seller ➔ Name the Merchant 
➔ Complete Purgatory / Crusades ➔ Re-defeat 4 Bishops ➔ Claim God of Death Fleece
```

### 1. Striking the Divine Partnership
After your initial post-game conversation, embark on your first revised crusade. Defeating any mini-boss in the revamped zones rewards your first God Tear. Upon returning, the merchant acknowledges your feat and prompts you to bestow a custom name upon them. 

### 2. Liberating the Bishops
Your core quest involves venturing through Darkwood, Anura, Anchordeep, and the Silk Cradle once more. The bishops—Leshy, Heket, Kallamar, and Shamura—now possess amplified health pools, aggressive attack chains, and supplementary hazards.

| Realm | Bishop Boss | Unlocked Follower Trait | Post-Defeat Dialogue Theme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Darkwood** | Leshy (Rematch) | Chaos & Blind Devotion | Chaotic early ascension |
| **Anura** | Heket (Rematch) | Ruthless Hunger | Fierce, vicious nature |
| **Anchordeep** | Kallamar (Rematch) | Cowardice & Swiftness | Stubborn refusal to listen |
| **Silk Cradle** | Shamura (Rematch) | Tactical Wisdom | Tragic intellectual fall |

### 3. Claiming the Ultimate Boons
After subduing all four deities and returning to the base hub, speak with the named merchant. Depending on how you resolved your confrontation with The One Who Waits during the main campaign, you receive distinct rewards:
- **Spared The One Who Waits**: Grants **The One Who Waits Trophy** decoration blueprint and the **God of Death Fleece**.
- **Executed The One Who Waits**: The vendor delivers Narinder directly to you as a permanent follower in his exclusive follower form, alongside the **God of Death Fleece**.

## Farming and Spending God Tears Efficiently

The primary loop of this **Infant God walkthrough** focuses on collecting God Tears to spin the merchant's prize wheel. God Tears serve as the premier post-game currency for unlocking missing doctrines, exclusive follower skins, and potent relics.

### Sources of God Tears
- **Crusade Mini-Bosses & Bosses**: Slaying any enhanced crusade boss drops a guaranteed God Tear.
- **Purgatory Mode**: Successfully clearing a daily run through Purgatory yields Tears without advancing base time.
- **Loyalty Level-Ups**: Leveling up high-devotion followers past their soft cap provides additional tear shards.

| Wheel Reward Item | Base Probability | Drop Pool Limitation | Purpose / Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forgotten Commandment Stone** | ~40% (Initial) | Max 24 (28 with DLC) | Unlock mutually exclusive Cult Doctrines |
| **Special Follower Forms** | ~20% | Exhaustible pool | Unlocks cosmetic forms (e.g., Koala, Shrimp) |
| **Base Decorations** | ~20% | Exhaustible pool | Cosmic and holy decorative blueprints |
| **Holy Talisman Shards** | ~20% | Exhaustible pool | Used to unlock remaining Fleeces |
| **Rare Necklaces** | Replaces cleared slots | Infinite supply | Grants immortality, demonic bonuses, or loyalty buffs |

> **Pro Tip (Player Experience):** According to community reports and player testing, you can save your game right in front of the Mystic Seller before handing over a God Tear. If the wheel lands on an item you do not currently need, quit to the main menu and reload to reroll your reward without losing your currency.

## Combat Strategy for Post-Game Bishop Rematches

During this stage of the **Infant God walkthrough**, combat difficulty spikes considerably. Enemies hit harder, bullet-hell patterns overlap, and boss arenas frequently spawn explosive modifiers.

### Optimized Post-Game Build Recommendations
To make quick work of the revamped Bishops, prepare your build before stepping into the teleporter.

| Build Element | Recommended Choice | Strategic Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fleece** | Fleece of the Fates / Golden Fleece | Guarantees instant Tarot synergy or scales damage on hitless runs |
| **Primary Weapon** | Godly Axe / Vampiric Blunderbuss | High base burst damage or sustained lifesteal during long battles |
| **Curse** | Hounds of Fate / Divine Blast | Long-range tracking projectiles or invulnerability frames against swarms |
| **Demon Followers** | Vesta (Fervor) + Paean (Extra Hearts) | Ensures constant spellcasting while mitigating incoming boss burst |

### Key Rematch Tactics
1. **Manage Minion Spawns Instantly**: In the revamped encounters, adds spawn with elite modifiers. Use wide-area curses to clear them before they clutter your dodging lanes.
2. **Prioritize I-Frames**: Boss projectile density is nearly double that of the base campaign. Rely on roll invulnerability rather than simply running away from targeted bursts.
3. **Exploit Purgatory for Practice**: Take advantage of the daily Purgatory runs. Time stands completely still for your cult while you run Purgatory, allowing you to master boss attack rhythms without starving your followers back home.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What triggers the "Infant God" questline in Cult of the Lamb?
The questline begins immediately after you defeat The One Who Waits in the main storyline. Returning to the crusade gateway will reveal the Mystic Seller (`???`), who addresses you as an "infant god" and unlocks the post-game crusade rematches.

### How many Forgotten Commandment Stones can I get from the Mystic Seller?
In the base version of the game, you can obtain up to 24 Forgotten Commandment Stones from the Mystic Seller. If you have installed major expansions like the Woolhaven DLC, this cap increases to 28 stones, allowing you to unlock every single mutually exclusive doctrine for your cult.

### Can I recruit all four Bishops as followers during this walkthrough?
Yes. Following this **Infant God walkthrough** and defeating each Bishop a second time in their respective crusade zones will purify their spirits. Upon defeat, they will appear back at your cult grounds as unique, legendary followers sporting their custom follower skins.

### What happens if I run out of God Tears when talking to the vendor?
If you interact with the Mystic Seller without any God Tears in your inventory, they will dismiss you with stern dialogue warning you not to cheat them. No negative status effects occur; simply complete another crusade run or clear Purgatory to earn more Tears before returning.
