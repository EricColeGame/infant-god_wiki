## Core Mechanics: Understanding Divine Evolution and Essence

In *Infant God*, your character starts in a fragile juvenile state, gradually absorbing divine sparks, managing worship devotion, and unlocking celestial abilities. Unlike traditional action-RPG protagonists, your strength relies on two distinct pillars: Divine Essence (your character's internal stats and skills) and Realm Devotion (your external influence and passive power generation).

```
[Divine Sparks] ──> [Essence Refinement] ──> [Domain Ascendancy]
       │
       └──> [Shrine Cultivation] ──> [Passive Realm Devotion]
```

To maximize your efficiency during the opening acts, monitor your three primary resources:

1. **Divine Essence (DE):** Spent on active skill upgrades, domain traits, and stat allocations.
2. **Faith Shards:** Gathered from defeated guardians and sanctified altars, used to awaken ancient relics.
3. **Anima Flux:** Your real-time resource during combat, dictating spell casting and evasive maneuvers.

### Essential Resource Management Matrix

| Resource Type | Primary Source | Optimal Usage | Common Beginner Mistake |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Divine Essence** | Boss encounters, quest milestones | Unlocking tier-1 passives, core stat scaling | Spreading points across opposing elemental affinities |
| **Faith Shards** | Cleansing tainted altars, side shrines | Upgrading early-game relic slots | Hoarding them past Act 1 without unlocking relic passives |
| **Anima Flux** | Passive combat regen, perfect dodges | Chaining burst spells, defensive parries | Depleting the entire pool on basic crowd-control spells |
| **Celestial Embers** | Daily trial rifts, rare elites | Transmuting gear affinities | Using them on temporary starter gear |

## Early Game Progression: Your First 10 Hours

The opening chapters are designed to test your adaptability. Following a structured leveling pathway prevents hitches against the notoriously brutal Act 1 gatekeeper bosses. This **Infant God beginner guide** outlines the ideal sequence for tackling early regions.

```
Awakening Sanctum ──> Verdant Hollows ──> Sunken Crypts ──> Sunken Citadel (Boss)
```

### Recommended Chapter 1 Progression Route

| Zone Name | Recommended Level | Key Objectives | Notable Drops / Rewards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Awakening Sanctum** | Levels 1–3 | Complete the awakening trial, attune first domain | *Shattered Halo Relic*, Basic Divine Sparks |

### Critical Milestones to Hit Before Level 10

* **Unlock your Secondary Affinity:** At level 6, visit the Grand Pantheon to bind a complementary element.
* **Establish your First Sanctuary:** Sanctuaries provide passive item refinement while exploring hostile zones.
* **Socket Your Initial Relics:** Never leave relic slots empty; even flat health or defense bonuses drastically improve early survivability.

Your equipment power is directly tied to the Sanctification system. Instead of constantly replacing weapons every few levels, *Infant God* encourages players to upgrade their favorite armaments by infusing them with elemental catalysts.

### Sanctification Upgrade Tiers

| Gear Tier | Required Catalyst | Success Rate | Added Benefit / Modifier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Top 7 Essential Tips for New Players

To ensure a smooth journey through the early acts, keep these practical rules in mind as you explore the world:

1. **Never Neglect Altar Purification:** Purifying every corrupted shrine grants permanent stat bonuses that accumulate rapidly across zones.
2. **Specialize Early, Diversify Later:** Avoid splitting your Divine Essence among three or more elemental paths during your first 20 levels.
3. **Practice Perfect Parries:** Perfect parries cost zero Anima Flux and open enemies up to devastating critical retribution strikes.
4. **Use Consumable Elixirs:** Do not hoard your basic healing and flux elixirs; they reset upon resting at any sanctuary.
5. **Inspect Enemy Weaknesses:** Elemental affinities matter—hitting a stone-skinned golem with lightning yields far lower damage than striking with crushing earth or holy magic.
6. **Maintain Equipment Weight:** Keep your gear load under 70% to preserve fast roll animations and maintain optimal invulnerability frames.
7. **Revisit Cleared Areas:** Unlocking new mobility skills (such as the Astral Leap) allows you to reach previously inaccessible treasure vaults in starter zones.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most forgiving class for a newcomer in this Infant God beginner guide?
The Radiant Vanguard is universally considered the most forgiving archetype. Its innate self-sustain, high damage reduction, and generous parry windows allow new players to learn enemy attack patterns without suffering instant defeats.

### Can I respec my Divine Essence and skill points if I make a mistake?
Yes. Once you reach the Grand Sanctuary in Act 1, you can speak with the High Arbiter to reset your attribute distribution and skill tree allocations in exchange for a small amount of Faith Shards.

### How do I increase my maximum Anima Flux capacity?
Your Anima Flux pool expands primarily through leveling up your Spirit and Anima stats, equipping specific Blessed Relics, and claiming permanent bonuses from purified Celestial Altars scattered across the world.

### Does gear scale with player level or upgrade rank?
Gear scaling in *Infant God* relies primarily on its Sanctification Tier and your character's corresponding attribute ratings (such as Divine Might or Spell Focus), rather than your raw character level. Upgrading your weapon at the forge yields immediate and noticeable power spikes.
