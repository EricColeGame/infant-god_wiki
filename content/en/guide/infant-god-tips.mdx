## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most important Infant God tips for complete beginners?
The most critical beginner advice is focusing on stamina conservation and establishing structured rest routines. Rather than rushing high-tier encounters, prioritize building your defensive baseline, learning reaction management, and asking for support or co-op help whenever hitting a progression wall.

### How do I recover when my character build falls behind the power curve?
If your build feels underpowered, reset your skill allocation to focus heavily on a single primary attribute rather than spreading stats evenly. Focus on low-risk resource gathering loops to upgrade foundational equipment before re-attempting major trials.

### When is the best time to call for external help or co-op assists?
Call for assists as soon as an encounter threatens to completely drain your primary consumable stock. Using support mechanics early preserves valuable progression materials and allows you to learn encounter patterns safely.
